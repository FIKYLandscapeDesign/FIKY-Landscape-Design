How We Work

The Design Process

A clear, collaborative process from initial conversation to contractor handover.

  1. 01

    Meet & Brief

    Every project begins with an initial conversation to understand your site, your ideas and the way you want to live outdoors. We take time to explore the opportunities of your property, your architectural style and any practical considerations. This stage helps establish a clear design brief and ensures we are aligned before any design work begins.

  2. 02

    Concept Development

    Once the brief is established, we begin developing the concept design. This stage explores the overall layout, spatial flow, planting direction and materiality of the landscape. Concepts are presented for review, where we collaborate to refine and adjust the design until it feels right.

  3. 03

    Designs Delivered

    After concept approval, we develop the design into a detailed and build-ready package. This typically includes scaled plans, planting design, material selection guidance, and layout details for key elements such as pools, structures, paths and outdoor living zones.

  4. 04

    Handover to Contractor

    Once documentation is complete, we prepare the design for handover to your selected landscaper or builder. We can recommend trusted contractors if required and remain available to answer design-related questions during pricing and construction. This ensures the integrity of the design is carried through to completion on site.
